05
I DO · WHAT A SKILL ACTUALLY IS

A skill is a recipe card the AI must follow

--- name: announcement-thread
trigger: "/announcement-thread <topic>" ---

1. Pull the house structure: hook / three beats / CTA
LEARNED 07-12 · voice is warm, never formal. NEVER hashtags. 2. Draft two options: warmer + punchier
LEARNED 07-18 · the CTA links the blog FIRST, socials second. 3. Output both, ask which to polish
The highlighted lines are your corrections. They moved out of your memory and into the recipe. You will never type them again.
The margin notes are dated scars. Like a chef's notebook: "salt the water" gets written down the day it was forgotten, and is never forgotten again.
It is a plain text file. You can read it, edit it, share it. No code required.

08
WE DO · HABIT TWO · LEAVE A NOTE FOR TOMORROW-YOU

Two Monday mornings

Without the note
monday, 9:02am
Hi! What would you like to work on today?
ok so… last week we drafted the thread, then there were tone notes, and that doc… let me go find it…
Happy to help! Could you share more context?
15 minutes re-explaining. Every session. Forever.
With the note
monday, 9:02am · note auto-loaded
RESUME → thread v2 sent for review
OPEN → waiting on the tone pick (opt 1 vs 2)
NEXT → schedule the follow-up
 
you: "continue"
0 minutes. It reads its own note and starts.

The note is three lines at the end of each session: done / open / next. Like a restaurant's closing checklist: two minutes at close, and the kitchen opens itself tomorrow.

WHY THIS IS WHERE WORK IS GOING
The scarce human input collapses to judgment and taste, supplied once, made permanent by machinery.

Execution, verification, follow-through, vigilance: infrastructure. What stays human is deciding what "good" means. The loop is how you only ever have to say it once. A personal library of skills and rules will be as normal as a contact list. Yours starts with one recipe card.
